Anonymous usage counts

To see what the app is used for, it sends anonymous counts about once a day: which sections were opened, how many things were played and for how many minutes by kind (film, episode, live channel, sport, esports), which features were used, the kind of device, the app's version, and roughly how it is set up (signed in or not, which debrid service, about how many addons). While you watch one of the app's own live channels, the app tells the server so, and the channel can show how many are watching.

None of it says who you are or what you watched: no account, no device ID, no titles, no searches, no addon addresses, and nothing about channels you made yourself. The counts are added to daily totals and your IP address is not stored. You can turn this off in the app, in Settings → About. The privacy page has the detail.

What is popular

If you sign in, the watch progress and My list your account syncs are also added up with everyone else's, to see which titles, genres and kinds of thing are watched and when. Only totals are looked at, never one account's history. Using the app without an account keeps what you watch on your device.

Removal

Accounts may be removed for abuse. You can delete your own account at any time from the home page.
