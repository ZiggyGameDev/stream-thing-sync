Privacy

Last updated 29 October 2026

stream-thing sync keeps your stream-thing app in step across your own phones, TVs and PCs. This page says, in plain words, what it keeps and why.

What is stored

  • From “Sign in with Google” (in the stream-thing app or on this website): your Google account ID, email address, name and profile picture link. Nothing else from your Google account is requested.
  • The API keys you choose to save (TorBox, Real-Debrid, MDBList, aioratings poster key) and which debrid service you use. Keys are encrypted before they are written to disk.
  • Your watch progress: which videos you've watched, how far through you are, and when.
  • The names of the devices you sign in to the app on, and when each was signed in and last used.

Why

Only so you can sign in on another of your devices and pick up your keys and progress there. Nothing else.

Sharing

Your data is not shared with anyone and is never sold.

Cookies and tracking

There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics. The website sets one cookie: a session cookie that keeps you signed in. Google's sign-in button is loaded from Google, which handles the sign-in itself under Google's own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Sign in on this site and choose “Delete account”. Your account, saved keys, watch progress, devices and sessions are deleted immediately. You can also sign out individual devices at any time.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/ZiggyGameDev/stream-thing-sync.
